Play overview
Positioning statement
Automation Anywhere and Blue Prism customers are sitting on aging RPA infrastructure with no clear agentic AI roadmap. Meanwhile, their competitors are accelerating. UiPath offers the only enterprise-grade path from legacy task automation to autonomous AI agents — with a proven migration playbook, dedicated CoE tooling, and the largest RPA-to-AI partner ecosystem in the market.
Target qualification signals
AA / Blue Prism renewal Agentic AI initiative started Platform consolidation project New CTO / Head of Automation 500+ automations in prod CoE under review Cost reduction mandate
Common objections & responses
  • "We've invested too much in AA/BP to switch" — Quantify stranded cost vs. migration ROI; highlight reference customers who switched
  • "Migration is too risky" — Lead with phased migration path, UiPath Migration Accelerator, and dedicated PS resources
  • "AA has agentic AI too" — Demo TARA + Document Understanding + Autopilot head-to-head; lean on Forrester/Gartner positioning
Key competitive differentiators
  • Only platform with native agentic AI (TARA), document understanding, and process mining in one unified suite
  • Migration Accelerator: automated conversion of AA/BP bots with 80%+ code reuse rate
  • G2 & Forrester Wave leader 5 years running — strongest enterprise proof points
  • Platform TCO advantage: avg 34% lower 3-yr cost vs. AA Enterprise per IDC study
Personas & discovery questions
4 personas
⚙️
Head of Automation / CTO
VP/Director · Technical champion or economic buyer
Owns the automation roadmap. Motivated by platform capability, vendor support quality, and roadmap confidence. Frustrated by AA/BP's slow release cycles. Excited by agentic AI but skeptical of vendor hype.
Discovery questions
  • 01Where does your current automation platform fall short of where you need to be in 18–24 months, especially around AI and agentic workflows?
  • 02How would you describe the innovation velocity of your current vendor — are they keeping pace with what the business needs?
  • 03What does your migration risk tolerance look like? If you had a 90-day phased migration path with guaranteed bot conversion, would that change your calculus?
  • 04Tell me about your CoE structure — how centralized is it, and how well is it positioned to manage a platform transition?
🔧
CoE Lead / RPA Program Manager
Director/Senior Manager · Day-to-day platform owner
Responsible for bot uptime, developer productivity, and CoE maturity. Concerned about migration overhead and team retraining. Motivated by better tooling, governance dashboards, and reducing manual ops work.
Discovery questions
  • 01How much of your team's time goes to maintaining existing bots vs. building new automations? What would ideal look like?
  • 02What's your developer experience like on your current platform — bot studio, testing, debugging, deployment pipelines?
  • 03If you were to migrate to a new platform, what would your team's biggest day-1 concern be?
  • 04How are you managing governance and bot performance monitoring today — is that a manual process or do you have tooling?
💼
CFO / VP Finance
C-suite · Economic approver or blocker
Needs a clear ROI narrative and cost-avoidance story. Responds to benchmark data and peer comparisons. Skeptical of platform migrations unless quantified savings are substantial.
Discovery questions
  • 01What is your fully-loaded annual cost of your current automation platform — licensing, infrastructure, support, and internal FTE overhead?
  • 02Have you modeled the cost of staying on your current platform for another 3 years, including renewal escalations and tech debt?
  • 03What payback period and ROI threshold would make a platform migration a straightforward approval for you?
📋
COO / Head of Operations
C-suite · Business outcomes owner
Focused on productivity gains, headcount leverage, and operational resilience. Wants automation to scale with business growth. Cares about agentic AI as a competitive differentiator.
Discovery questions
  • 01How many FTEs have you effectively displaced or redeployed through automation in the last 2 years? How does that compare to your target?
  • 02Where are the highest-value automation opportunities still sitting unrealized in your org — and what's blocking them?
  • 03If your automation platform could handle end-to-end agentic workflows — not just task automation — how would that change your operational roadmap?
